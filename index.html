The Three-Outcome Distinction

A rigorous understanding of venture fundraising requires separating three distinct categories: Socializing (which generates dialogue and relationships), Fundraising Execution (which produces deliverables and outreach work), and Capitalization (which deposits usable capital on the startup balance sheet).

Progress in one category must never be automatically conflated with progress in another.
